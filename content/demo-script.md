## Recording notes

- **Capture.** QuickTime screen recording at 1080p, Chrome full screen, cream page only. Hide the debug transcript line on `/read` for every take. Mute system sounds other than the Alder clips.
- **One take per day.** Record Day 1, Day 2 and Day 5 as separate recordings and reload between them, so every cut between days hides load time and the `/api/next-page` round trip. The "Next page" click at the end of Day 2 is the only click that crosses a day boundary, and the cut lands right after it.
- **The reader.** A team member reads off-camera into the laptop mic at a seven-year-old's pace. On Day 1 they deliberately go silent right before "through", "think" and "there" so the 2.5 s stumble timer fires; after each clip they say the word clearly and keep going. On Day 2 they pause before the first "through", read it themselves from the pieces, and read everything else clean. Day 5 is read clean start to finish, a little quicker. Silence while looking at the word is what the matcher expects; a soft "th..." that does not match is fine too.
- **Hint ladder on screen.** The shot list assumes `/read` plays the hint rung recorded in `content/sessions.json` for Day 1 (whole word on "through", first piece on "think", pieces then the card on "there"). If the build plays the same clips on every stumble (chunks, then sound-out), record it as it is and use `soundOut.think` in the 0:26 shot. The beat is the same. If a sound-out fires on "there" before the card, cut it.
- **Hidden-hotkey fallback.** If speech recognition misbehaves on the take (Web Speech API is unreliable on quiet voices), switch `/read` to demo mode: spacebar advances the underline, a hidden hotkey fires a stumble, a second one fires the flag. The video is identical. Log it in the README as "demo mode" per HACKATHON_CONTEXT section 8.
- **Fallback pages are fine.** The video reads the hand-written passages (`content/passages.json`). The narration describes the system, not the page shown. The README GIF carries the one live generation.
- **Stalls.** Every 2.5 s stall is recorded in full and trimmed to about 1 s (or a beat) in the edit. Never talk over a Alder clip; duck the reader under the narrator everywhere except where the shot list says "not ducked".
- **Parent view.** Use `/parent?day=5`: at day 5 the page shows through, think and there as clicked and where, which as working on, with Monday 6, Wednesday 9, Friday 11. At `?day=7`, "which" moves to clicked and the on-screen text should follow the page; the narration does not name the words, so it survives either way.
- **B-roll list.** (1) Laptop on a kitchen table with the Day 5 page on it, mug or cereal box in frame, afternoon light, slow push-in. (2) A hand turning a page of a paperback beside the laptop. (3) The parent view on a phone held over the table, one slow thumb scroll. A hand or a shoulder is enough; no face is needed. **No AI-generated child footage,** and no child on camera without a parent's yes.
- **Edit.** CapCut or iMovie. Lower-thirds "Day 1 · Monday", "Day 2 · Tuesday", "Day 5 · Friday" in small Lexend, bottom left, fading after 2 s. Black open card and cream title card are editor cards, not screens in the app.
- **Voiceover clips.** `npx tsx scripts/record-voiceover.ts --force` regenerates `public/audio/vo/<id>.mp3` from `content/voiceover.ts` once the narrator voice is chosen. If the TTS mangles "Edtech", change the text to "Ed tech" in `voiceover.ts` and re-run.
