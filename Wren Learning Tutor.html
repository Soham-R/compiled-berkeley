    Dyslexia support

    Reading that works with a dyslexic brain

    Choose "Dyslexia or reading difficulty" in the form and Wren changes how every reading activity looks and sounds.

    bed
    • Sounds before letters. Lots of rhyme and first-sound play before reading words.
    • Easy-to-read letters. A clear font, extra space between letters and words, and a warm, low-glare page.
    • b, d, p and q in their own colours. They are never shown as each other's wrong answers.
    • A focus window when reading aloud. Only the next few words are bright, so the eyes don't jump ahead.
    • More time and fewer choices. Wren waits longer before helping and shows 2 choices instead of 3.
    • Every word can be heard. Tap any word to hear it sounded out, then read it out loud.
