Made for how they learn

The same lesson, shaped for your child

Sensitive to soundA slow, low voice, no sudden sound effects, and volume that fades in.
ADHD traitsShort, quick rounds built around their favorite topic, with frequent small wins.
Speech delayAnswer by tapping. Sound play like "bzzz" and "choo choo" before letter shapes.
Hard transitionsA visual bar and a short heads-up before switching activities.
Dislikes being timedNo countdowns. Wren waits, and quietly simplifies if your child is stuck.
Seeks bold visualsHigh-contrast outlines and larger, clearer objects.

For parents

Always know how your child is doing

Daily or weekly reports

A short summary in plain language, progress on your goals, and one thing to try at home.

Real mastery tracking

A skill counts as learned only when your child gets it right on different days, not just once.

Private by design

Wren doesn't diagnose. Your child's data stays yours, and you can delete it at any time.
